# AgriTek interview and pilot scorecard

Use this script to validate the raspberry exception desk. Do not pitch for the
first 20 minutes. Ask for recent facts, documents and costs—not opinions about a
hypothetical marketplace.

## Buyer / importer-repacker interview

1. Walk through the last load that arrived late, short or outside specification.
2. What triggered the supply gap, and how many hours did you have to replace it?
3. Who found the replacement, and how were supplier, certification and bank
   details verified?
4. Which exact documents or analyses most often arrive late or wrong?
5. At what point can you still cancel, substitute origin or change pack format?
6. How do origin and arrival inspections differ, and who wins when they conflict?
7. What did the last rejected or discounted load cost in product, labour,
   customer penalties and management time?
8. What payment terms can an unfamiliar exporter realistically receive?
9. Which suppliers/routes are excluded regardless of price, and why?
10. Would you expose one historical exception to a structured replay?
11. For a successful €50k–€100k replacement load, which is more acceptable:
    €1,000 fixed, 2% success fee, or paying each inspector/logistics vendor only?
12. Who else must approve a live pilot, and what proof do they require?

Ask for anonymized versions of the purchase specification, rejected-load report,
claim email chain, logger output and document checklist.

## Exporter / packhouse interview

1. Walk through the last load rejected, discounted, delayed or paid late.
2. What spot/backup volume can be sold outside programmed retail contracts?
3. Which farm, lot, pallet and analysis evidence exists before packing?
4. Which buyer specifications create the most rework or disputes?
5. How early can the commercial, phytosanitary, conformity and traceability file
   be complete?
6. Who controls the origin inspection and retained sample?
7. Which payment instrument is acceptable for a first-time Spanish buyer?
8. What receivable insurance/factoring is available and at what cost?
9. What information would you share with a buyer before naming the farm?
10. Would you run a paid pilot without AgriTek taking title or guaranteeing the
    buyer?

## Customs, logistics and inspection interview

1. Reconstruct the exact Portugal → Spain or Morocco → Algeciras → Andalusia
   critical path and cutoff times.
2. Which documents create holds, corrections or loss of preference most often?
3. When must TRACES/CHED-PP and control-point handling be initiated?
4. What evidence reliably distinguishes origin quality, transit damage and
   receiver handling?
5. What sampling plan and photo/logger package survives a commercial dispute?
6. What is the realistic price and lead time for origin and arrival inspection?
7. Where can one party alter a file or instruction without the other noticing?
8. What would make you refuse to participate in the pilot?

## Evidence score

Score each interview from 0–2 per row.

| Signal | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recent exception | none / vague | described | evidence shared |
| Costly pain | immaterial | annoying | quantified material loss |
| Urgency | no deadline | days | hours / customer at risk |
| Current solution | works well | fragmented | repeated failure |
| Buyer willingness | no payment | conditional | fee/range accepted |
| Pilot access | refused | replay only | named live candidate |
| Boundary fit | demands title/credit | unclear | accepts coordination-only |

Prioritize interviews scoring 10 or more out of 14. A compliment, waitlist signup
or request for a future demo is not validation.

## Pilot commitment

A valid buyer pilot commitment names:

- legal entity and authorized sponsor;
- target route/product and approximate load value;
- the live shortage/exception condition that activates it;
- documents and acceptance specification AgriTek may process;
- agreed fee hypothesis and third-party cost treatment;
- buyer responsibility for importer-of-record, customs and direct exporter
  payment; and
- the date on which the commitment expires.

## Falsifiers

Stop or pivot when, after 25 interviews:

- fewer than two buyers share genuine exception evidence;
- buyers require AgriTek to take title, provide credit or guarantee performance;
- no buyer accepts at least €750–€1,000 for a successful load;
- qualified packhouses cannot expose backup volume or objective lot evidence;
- inspection/logistics cost makes contribution negative; or
- no two buyers authorize a replay or controlled pilot.
